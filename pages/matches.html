How Skill Matching Works

Our client-side matching engine compares your teaching capabilities and learning goals with every peer on campus in 4 straightforward steps:

Step 1: Parse Skills

Extracts your offered skills and desired subjects from your published listings.

Step 2: Cross Compare

Performs case-insensitive substring matching between what you want and what peers offer.

Step 3: Score Compatibility

Assigns points using reciprocal barter rules (95, 75, or 65).

Step 4: Active Filter

Hides peers with an in-progress proposal so you only see actionable new partners.

Worked Example (Rahul & Aarav)

Rahul teaches Python and Web Frontend and wants Acoustic Guitar and Spanish Basics; Aarav teaches Acoustic Guitar and wants Web Frontend; result: mutual match scored 95 points.
